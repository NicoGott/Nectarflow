# Works this reproduction leans on

> Ford CR, Jones L (2020) *nbgw-plant-illumina-pipeline*. Zenodo.
> [record 4166248](https://zenodo.org/record/4166248) &mdash; the archived pipeline the paper's
> Methods cite for its sequence analysis, and so the provenance of the replica.
>
> Jones L, Brennan GL, Lowe A, Creer S, Ford CR, de Vere N (2021) Shifts in honeybee foraging
> reveal historical changes in floral resources. *Communications Biology* 4: 37.
> [doi:10.1038/s42003-020-01562-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-020-01562-4) &mdash; the
> 5,887-species reference library the paper used.
>
> Jones L, Twyford AD, Ford CR, Rich TCG, Davies H, Forrest LL, et al. (2021) Barcode UK: a
> complete DNA barcoding resource for the flowering plants and conifers of the United Kingdom.
> *Molecular Ecology Resources* 21: 2050-2062.
> [doi:10.1111/1755-0998.13388](https://doi.org/10.1111/1755-0998.13388) &mdash; the backbone of
> the library built here.
>
> Stroh PA, Walker KJ, Humphrey TA, Pescott OL, Burkmar RJ (2024) *Plant Atlas 2020: native status
> data*. Zenodo. [doi:10.5281/zenodo.11097371](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.11097371) &mdash;
> CC BY 4.0, and the target list the reference library is built from.
>
> Stace C (2019) *New Flora of the British Isles*, 4th ed. &mdash; the authors' native species
> list. Preston CD, Pearman DA, Dines TD (2002) *New Atlas of the British and Irish Flora*. OUP.
> &mdash; their naturalised and alien species. Both are books; the Plant Atlas 2020 stands in for
> them here, which is a substitution and not a reproduction.
>
> Lowe A (2022) *Investigating floral resource use by pollinators using pollen DNA metabarcoding*.
> PhD thesis, Bangor University. &mdash; carries this study as its chapter three, with the
> supporting information the journal placed in its appendices. Cited, not redistributed.
